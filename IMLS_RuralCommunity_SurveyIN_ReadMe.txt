RESEARCH AIMS and METHODS

The Social Service Model for Rural Libraries study aims to develop a social service prototype for rural libraries by collecting survey (quantitative) and interview (qualitative) data from rural libraries in Indiana, and then expanding to rural counties across the country. The goal is to develop a prototype model that could be validated on a national scale, and which can be a useful resource/tool for rural libraries to meet community needs. During phase 1, data was collected with Qualtrics from over 1,000 online survey respondents in rural Indiana Counties, and then 11 in-depth interviews were conducted with library leadership from the most responsive rural counties.
